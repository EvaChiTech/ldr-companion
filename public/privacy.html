Privacy

Last updated: edit this date. This is a starter template — please tailor to your jurisdiction and use a lawyer for production.

What we store

When you create or join a room, we store the names, timezones, and shared data you enter (notes, messages, photos, voice notes, dreams, questions, expenses, etc.) in our database. This data is keyed to a room code that only you and your partner know.

If you sign in with email, we store your email address (managed by Supabase Auth) and a list of room codes you've joined to keep your devices in sync.

What we don't store

  • We never store payment information — when you "Send something" via local services, you complete the order on the service's own site.
  • We don't sell your data. Ever.
  • We don't have a marketing team to spam you.

AI features

When you use AI features (date-night suggestions, daily questions, memory book, tone translator, etc.), the relevant data is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the response. Anthropic's privacy practices apply to that processing.

Notifications

If you enable browser notifications, we store the push-subscription endpoint your browser issues. We use it only to send you notifications about activity in your rooms.

Analytics

We track basic feature usage (which tabs you visit, when you create a room, etc.) to learn what to improve. No message content, photo content, or personal text is included in analytics events.

Deleting your data

You can leave any room at any time. To request full deletion, email us at the address in the README.
